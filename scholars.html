Sayyid Ali Imran

Sayyid Ali studied in the seminary of Qom from 2012 to 2021, while also concurrently obtaining a M.A in Islamic Studies from the Islamic College of London in 2018. He completed his Master of Education at the University of Toronto in 2025 and is currently working as the principal of Al Haadi School in Toronto.

Ustadha Zermina Awan

Sr. Zermina Awan was born and raised in the UK and pursued her seminary education at Jamiat al-Zahra in Qom, specializing in Islamic theology (kalam). She also holds a postgraduate degree in Religion and Politics from the University of Birmingham.

She taught Islamic theology, Islamic history, and fiqh at the Hawza Ilmiyyah of England from 2014–2019, later serving with Al-Kisa Foundation and the 2nd Chance Project. She is currently an instructor at Risalat Institute U.K. under the leadership of Shaykh Mohammed Ali Shomali, and regularly delivers workshops and lectures in both English and Urdu on a wide range of topics.
